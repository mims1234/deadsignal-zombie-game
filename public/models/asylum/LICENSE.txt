The Asylum's real-asset people (halloway.vrm and any others here) are built by scripts/asylum-cast.py in Blender
with the MPFB add-on from MakeHuman's system assets (bodies, skins, eyes, hair, clothes), which MakeHuman released
under CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The nurse's cap, the prisoner's pinafore and the recolouring
are made by the script. These files are released under CC0 1.0 as well.
MakeHuman: http://www.makehumancommunity.org
