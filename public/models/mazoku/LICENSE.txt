The Mazoku card carriers' real-asset models (shiroko, sofia, mahiru, noire, miku .vrm, worn with NEW 3D MODELS) were
built in Blender with the VRM add-on from VRoid Studio sample models by VRoid Project / pixiv: the CC0 HairSample_Female
body and face (https://opengameart.org/content/vroid-studio-cc0-models), and the hair of the Sendagaya Shino sample,
whose VRoid Hub licence allows modification, redistribution and commercial use without credit. The garments, faces'
zombie touch, recolouring and accessories were made for this game. Credit to VRoid Project / pixiv is given anyway.
